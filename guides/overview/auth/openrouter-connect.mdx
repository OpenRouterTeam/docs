OpenRouter Connect lets a platform provision OpenRouter for its own customers. Instead of asking each customer to sign up, add a payment method, and create keys, your parent organization creates a customer organization on their behalf and receives a management key scoped to it. You can then create API keys, set limits, and inspect usage for that customer from your own product.

Your customers configure model and provider access, budgets, and data policies in your product, and OpenRouter enforces those controls on your behalf and provides the trust and safety layer underneath. OpenRouter bills your parent organization, and you bill your customers.

Connect has three goals.

- Give every customer their own OpenRouter organization, so usage and keys stay separate per customer while billing goes to your parent organization.
- Let your platform create those customer organizations programmatically or from the dashboard, without a manual signup flow for each customer.
- Hand your platform a management key for each customer organization, so your backend can manage that customer's configuration and API keys through the [Management API](/guides/overview/auth/management-api-keys).

<Warning>
Connect is not enabled by default. The OpenRouter team must manually grant the Connect entitlement to each parent organization that will create customer organizations. Until the entitlement is granted, the parent organization cannot view the Connect page or create customer organizations through the dashboard or the API. Contact your OpenRouter account team to request access.
</Warning>

## Requirements to use Connect

- Your parent account must be an OpenRouter organization. Personal accounts cannot use Connect.
- The parent organization must have the Connect entitlement, granted by OpenRouter.
- To use the Connect dashboard, you must be an admin of the parent organization.
- To use the Connect API to create customer organizations, you need a [management API key](https://openrouter.ai/settings/management-keys) created in the parent organization.

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Parent organization | Your own OpenRouter organization. It holds the Connect entitlement and creates customer organizations. |
| Customer organization | A dedicated OpenRouter organization for one of your customers, created by your parent organization. |
| Connect management key | Any management key created in your parent organization. It creates customer organizations. |
| Customer management key | A management key scoped to one customer organization, returned when that organization is created. It manages everything inside that customer organization. |

## How customer organizations work

Each customer organization is owned by a managed user created from the owner email you provide. The customer organization keeps the customer name you provide, for example `Acme Corp`. When someone is signed in to a customer organization, the OpenRouter header shows a `Connect org · Your Organization` badge, where `Your Organization` is the name of your parent organization.

The customer name identifies the customer within your parent organization. Names are compared after trimming whitespace and ignoring case, so `Acme Corp` and ` acme corp ` refer to the same customer. Creating the same customer again never creates a duplicate. If a management key for that customer was already delivered, the request fails with `409` and the message "This customer organization already exists".

Usage in a customer organization is billed to your parent Connect organization. You do not need to add credits to each customer organization.

Your Connect management key creates customer organizations but cannot manage anything inside them. Use each customer management key for that. A customer management key is an ordinary management key for its customer organization. It cannot create further customer organizations. You can create more management keys inside a customer organization, for example to replace a lost or rotated key.

## Create a parent organization in the dashboard

1. Sign in to OpenRouter and go to [Preferences](https://openrouter.ai/settings/preferences).
2. In the **Organization** row, click **Create** and enter a name for your parent organization. If you already belong to an organization, the row shows **Manage** instead of **Create**. In that case, you can use an organization you are an admin of as your parent organization.
3. Switch to the new parent organization.
4. Ask your OpenRouter account team to grant the Connect entitlement to the parent organization. The [Connect page](https://openrouter.ai/settings/connect) shows "Page not found" until the entitlement is granted.

## Create a customer organization with the API

Create a [management key](https://openrouter.ai/settings/management-keys) while the parent organization is selected. Any management key created in the parent organization is a Connect management key.

Send a `POST` request to `/api/v1/organization` with your Connect management key.

```bash
curl https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/organization \
  -X POST \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENROUTER_MANAGEMENT_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Acme Corp",
    "email": "owner@acme.example"
  }'
```

### Request body

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | string | Customer organization name. Must contain letters or numbers and be at most 256 characters. |
| `email` | string | Email address for the managed owner of the customer organization. |

No other fields are accepted.

### Response

A successful request returns `200` with the customer organization, the grant that links it to your parent organization, and a management key for the customer organization.

```json
{
  "organization": {
    "id": "org_...",
    "name": "Acme Corp",
    "slug": "...",
    "email": "owner@acme.example"
  },
  "grant": {
    "id": "...",
    "scopes": [
      "inference",
      "keys_read",
      "keys_write",
      "workspaces_read",
      "workspaces_write",
      "organization_members_read"
    ]
  },
  "management_key": {
    "name": "...",
    "key": "..."
  },
  "created": true
}
```

- `management_key` contains the plaintext management key for the customer organization. Every successful response includes one. Store the key securely as soon as you receive it, because OpenRouter cannot show it again.

Use the returned management key to call the [Management API](/guides/overview/auth/management-api-keys) on behalf of the customer organization, for example to create API keys for it.

### Errors

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `400` | The request body is invalid, for example a missing or empty `name` or an invalid `email`. |
| `401` | The management key is missing or invalid. |
| `403` | The key is not a management key, the parent account is not an organization, or the parent organization does not have Connect enabled. |
| `409` | The request conflicts with existing state, for example the customer organization already exists and its management key was already delivered, an account already exists for the owner email, the customer organization was deleted, or provisioning for the same customer is already in progress. |
| `429` | Too many requests. Wait and retry. |
| `500` | Unexpected error. Retrying the same request is safe. |

## Create a customer organization in the dashboard

1. Sign in to OpenRouter and switch to your parent organization.
2. Go to the [Connect page](https://openrouter.ai/settings/connect). If the page shows "Page not found", the parent organization does not have the Connect entitlement yet.
3. Click **New customer**.
4. Enter the **Organization name** and the **Owner email**. The owner email creates the managed owner account for the customer organization.
5. Click **Create organization**. You may be asked to verify your identity with multi-factor authentication first.
6. Copy the management key shown after creation and store it securely. It cannot be retrieved again.

The Connect page lists the customer organizations your parent organization has created.

## Configure the customer organization

Authenticate with the customer management key to call the [Management API](/guides/overview/auth/management-api-keys) as the customer organization. How you set up each customer organization depends on your product and your customer's use case.

- **Workspaces.** [Create workspaces](/api/api-reference/workspaces/create-a-workspace) to separate configuration for different projects, teams, or agents. See [Workspaces](/guides/features/workspaces).
- **Guardrails.** [Create guardrails](/api/api-reference/guardrails/create-a-guardrail) to apply budgets, data policies, model and provider allowlists, and sensitive information filtering to each workspace you create for the customer. See [Guardrails](/guides/features/guardrails).
- **BYOK credentials.** [Add provider credentials](/api/api-reference/byok/create-a-byok-provider-credential) if the customer has existing provider agreements with discounted rates or commitments.
- **API keys.** [Create API keys](/api/api-reference/api-keys/create-a-new-api-key) for the customer organization to use for inference. The plaintext key is returned only in the creation response, so store it securely. Set the optional [`external`](/api/api-reference/api-keys/create-a-new-api-key#body-external) object to associate the key with one of your end users. `external.user` is your identifier for the end user, used for attribution. `external.api_key` is an optional key you supply, at least 32 characters long, that OpenRouter stores only as a SHA-256 hash and never returns.

<Note>
Coming soon. API routes to register and manage your customers' end users are in development. Each end user will be able to have their own guardrails and budgets. Until these routes ship, identify end users with `external.user` on API keys and `user` on inference requests.
</Note>

### Create a customer organization API key

Authenticate with the customer management key to create an API key in the customer organization. Set `external.user` to your identifier for the end user who will use the key.

```bash
curl https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/keys \
  -X POST \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CUSTOMER_MANAGEMENT_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Acme Corp production",
    "limit": 100,
    "limit_reset": "monthly",
    "external": {
      "user": "acme_employee_1042"
    }
  }'
```

The response returns `201` with the plaintext key in `key` and the key's metadata in `data`. `data.external_user` echoes the `external.user` value you set. Store `key` securely, because OpenRouter cannot show it again.

```json
{
  "data": {
    "name": "Acme Corp production",
    "hash": "...",
    "limit": 100,
    "limit_reset": "monthly",
    "external_user": "acme_employee_1042",
    "workspace_id": "..."
  },
  "key": "sk-or-v1-..."
}
```

Pass `workspace_id` to create the key in a specific workspace you created for the customer. The ID comes from the [create a workspace](/api/api-reference/workspaces/create-a-workspace) response.

## Send inference requests

Send inference requests with an API key that belongs to the customer organization. Set `user` to your identifier for the customer's end user. OpenRouter applies the customer organization's controls, routes the request, and reports the cost in the response for both BYOK and non-BYOK requests.

```bash
curl https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CUSTOMER_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "openai/gpt-5-mini",
    "user": "acme_employee_1042",
    "messages": [{ "role": "user", "content": "Hello" }]
  }'
```

The customer organization comes from the API key, never from the request body. The same `user` value under two different customer organizations refers to two separate end users.

## Track usage

Read usage per customer organization to bill your customers.

- **Analytics API.** Call [`GET /api/v1/analytics/meta`](/api/api-reference/analytics/get-available-analytics-metrics-and-dimensions) for the supported metrics, dimensions, filters, and granularities, then [`POST /api/v1/analytics/query`](/api/api-reference/analytics/query-analytics-data) with the customer management key to query usage for that customer organization.
- **Broadcast.** Send traces from customer organization requests to your observability platform with [Broadcast](/guides/features/broadcast). [Create a destination](/api/api-reference/observability/create-an-observability-destination) in each customer organization with its customer management key.

## Billing

- OpenRouter bills your parent organization for all customers you create through Connect.
- Customer organizations hold no credits or payment methods of their own. Their usage accrues to your parent organization.
- You bill your customers on whatever pricing model you choose, using the per-customer usage you read from OpenRouter.

## Integration checklist

1. Create a parent organization and ask the OpenRouter team to grant the Connect entitlement.
2. Create a Connect management key in the parent organization and store it in your secrets manager.
3. Build the flow in your product that creates a customer organization for each new customer.
4. Store each customer management key securely, keyed by your customer identifier.
5. Configure each customer organization with the workspaces, guardrails, BYOK credentials, and API keys it needs.
6. Send inference requests with the customer organization's API key and set `user` to your end-user identifier.
7. Read usage from responses, the Analytics API, or Broadcast to bill your customers.
8. Pay your OpenRouter invoice.
