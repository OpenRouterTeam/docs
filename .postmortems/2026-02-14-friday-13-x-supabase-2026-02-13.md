*Friday 13 x Supabase 2026-02-13*

User impact
• Delayed generations API and activity page logs from 9:03AM to 3:32PM Pacific
• Budgets inconsistently enforced over the same period
• Slowness in chatroom and other app pages
Inference was _not_ impacted. Throughout the whole period *our generation APIs remained available*.

Root causes
• Suspicious new users did a large volume of BYOK requests (all to [Z.ai](http://Z.ai)), which put very high write volume on our main Supabase transactions table
• A long-running backup process contended with resources making it exceptionally slow for us to recover once the suspicious traffic was mitigated
