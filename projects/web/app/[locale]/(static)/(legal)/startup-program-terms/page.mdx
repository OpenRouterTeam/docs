To qualify, applicants must meet **all** eligibility criteria at the time of application and provide complete and accurate information. OpenRouter may request documentation or other information to verify eligibility and to reduce fraud and abuse.

Eligibility criteria may include (without limitation):

   - The applicant is a startup building venture-scalable, tech-related products full-time with an AI-native focus.
