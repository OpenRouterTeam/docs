简体中文（中国大陆）

- Use full-width punctuation: ，。：；！？（） and “ ” for quotes. Never mix half-width commas or periods into Chinese sentences. Latin product names, code, and numbers stay in half-width characters.
- No space between Chinese characters and adjacent Chinese punctuation. Keep a single half-width space on both sides of a Latin product name, model ID, or number inside a Chinese sentence: 在 OpenRouter 上、{count} 个模型.
- One rendering per product term across the whole batch. Guardrail is 护栏, Preset is 预设, Workspace is 工作区; never alternate with 防护栏, 防护规则, 预置, 工作空间.
- Labels are short noun phrases without a trailing 。 Buttons use verb + object: 新建护栏, 保存更改.
- Register is standard written Chinese for a technical product: 您 for the user in prose, no 您 in labels. No exclamation marks unless the source has one.
- Preferred product forms, use them in new translations even where existing copy still varies: 额度 for Credits as balance (余额 and 积分 are tolerated in existing copy, never 学分 or 致谢), 提供商 (Provider, 供应商 and 服务商 are tolerated in existing copy, never 提供者), 速率限制 (Rate limit, never 频率限制), 端点 (Endpoint, never 终结点 or 端口), API 密钥 with a space, 组织, 活动 (Activity), 日志 (Logs, 记录 is tolerated in existing copy), 网页搜索 (Web search, 网络搜索 is tolerated in existing copy, never 互联网搜索), 数据策略, 平台费 (Platform fee, never 平台手续费), 自动充值 (Auto top-up, never 自动续费).
- Interns and Auto Router stay in Latin script: "Ori 是一个 Intern", "创建 Interns". Never 实习生 or 自动路由器. Zero Data Retention stays in English with ZDR, and the gloss form is 零数据保留, never 零数据留存.
- Redact in guardrails is 脱敏 in every form: 脱敏 (Redact), 已脱敏 (Redacted), 脱敏规则 (redaction rules). Never 编辑, 修订, 删改, or 隐藏 for this action.
