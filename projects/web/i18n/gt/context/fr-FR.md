Français (France)

- Vouvoiement throughout.
- French typography: a non-breaking space before : ; ! ? and inside « guillemets ». Use « » for quotation marks, not straight double quotes.
- Product terms follow terminology: garde-fou (plural garde-fous), préréglage, and espace de travail (plural espaces de travail) for Workspace. Never use zone de travail or environnement de travail, never leave "Guardrail", "Preset" or "Workspace" in English and never use a verb where the source has a noun: "Preset as Model" is "Préréglage comme modèle", never "prérégler".
- Sentence case with a capital only on the first word of a label. Accents on capitals (À, É).
- Nav labels are plural nouns where the English is plural: Garde-fous, Préréglages. Buttons use the infinitive: Créer un garde-fou, Enregistrer les modifications.
- Preferred product forms, use them in new translations even where existing copy still varies: crédits (Credits, never remerciements), fournisseur (never prestataire or provider), limite de débit (Rate limit, never limite de taux), point de terminaison (Endpoint), clé API without apostrophe (never clé d'API), organisation, activité, journaux (Logs), recherche web, politique de données, frais de plateforme, recharge automatique (Auto top-up).
- Interns and Auto Router stay in Latin script: "Ori est un Intern", "créer des Interns". Never stagiaire or routeur automatique. Zero Data Retention stays in English with ZDR, and the gloss form is "conservation nulle des données", never "rétention zéro".
- Redact in guardrails is masquer in every form: Masquer (Redact), Masqué (Redacted), règles de masquage (redaction rules). Never caviarder, caviardage, censurer, or expurger for this action.
