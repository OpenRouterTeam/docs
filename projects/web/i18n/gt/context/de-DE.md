Deutsch (Deutschland)

- Sie-Form throughout. Never du.
- Instructions inside prose, help text, and errors use the Sie imperative (Vergleichen Sie, Wechseln Sie, Füllen Sie … aus), never the du imperative (Vergleiche, Wechsle, Fülle … aus).
- Product terms borrowed from English keep their English spelling and take German articles and plural endings: der Guardrail, die Guardrails, das Preset, die Presets, der Workspace. Do not translate them into German common nouns, and do not turn a noun into a verb: "Preset as Model" is "Preset als Modell", never "als Modell voreinstellen".
- Compounds with a protected or product term are hyphenated: Guardrail-Regel, Preset-Feld, Workspace-Einstellungen.
- Nouns are capitalized as German requires even inside a sentence-case label; everything else stays lowercase.
- German quotation marks are „ and “. No space before : ; ! ?
- Decimal numbers use the comma and a thin space before the percent sign: 99,9 %, not 99.9%.
- Prefer the short verb-final label style of German software for buttons: "Guardrail erstellen", "Änderungen speichern". Nav and tab labels are bare nouns: "Guardrails", "Presets", "Aktivität".
- Preferred product forms, use them in new translations even where existing copy still varies: Guthaben (Credits, never Gutschriften or Kredite), Anbieter (Provider), Rate-Limit with hyphen (Ratenlimit and Ratenbegrenzung are tolerated in existing copy, never Anfragelimit), Endpunkt, API-Schlüssel, Organisation, Aktivität, Protokolle (Logs), Websuche, Datenrichtlinie, Plattformgebühr, automatische Aufladung (Auto top-up). Fallback stays Fallback.
- Completion, when it names the model's output (prompt and completion, completion tokens, completion price, completion data, candidate completions), stays English with a German article and hyphenated compounds: die Completion, die Completions, Completion-Token, Completion-Preis, Completion-Daten. Never Abschluss, Vervollständigung or Antwort for the model output. The ordinary sense keeps its German word: "from request start to completion" is "bis zum Abschluss", and a chat completion notification is about an abgeschlossener Chat.
- Interns and Auto Router stay in Latin script: "Ori ist ein Intern", "Interns erstellen", "Auto-Router-Konfiguration" as a hyphenated compound. Never Praktikant or automatischer Router.
- Zero Data Retention stays in English with the ZDR abbreviation. Never "Keine Datenspeicherung".
- Redact in guardrails is schwärzen in every form: Schwärzen (Redact), Geschwärzt (Redacted), Schwärzungsregeln (redaction rules). Never redigieren, Redigierung, entfernen, or zensieren for this action.
