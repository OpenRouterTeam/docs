# Synapse — Verified Review Register, 2026-08-17

**Status:** findings register for the 2026-08-17 adversarially-verified review of the open stack, the deployed reviewer, and the plan
**Method:** two orchestrated passes (one reviewer agent per PR/doc target plus plan- and strategy-level lenses; then one adversarial verifier per target independently re-deriving every critical/major claim from the live PR heads). 68 serious claims were individually verified: **66 confirmed** (every G-row below), **2 refuted and excluded**. Minor findings were reported but not individually verified. The [2026-08-06 register](./review-findings.md) (F1–F19) was treated as settled; layers were checked for amendment **application status**, not re-litigated.
**Evidence:** every G-row links to its full claim, evidence, and verifier verdict in [`review-findings-2026-08-17-evidence.md`](./review-findings-2026-08-17-evidence.md) (in-repo). Raw agent transcripts additionally archived at `~/synapse-review-2026-08-17/` on the operator machine.

## How to use this register

Each finding needs a disposition before its layer's gate can be approved, with exactly the two options the manifest's §4 allows: **fix** (apply in the layer PR and link it here), or **re-disposition through the decision log** (amend `corpus-architecture.md` §19 with the reversal — an explicit, reasoned accept is recorded as a decision, never as a bare register note). Dispositions land in the last column in follow-up PRs — the same discipline as the F-register. G-numbers are stable identifiers.

## Amendment application status (summary)

The §4 merge-blocking amendments from the F-register are largely **not applied** at current PR heads: F7 (supersession) is absent from layers 1/3b/8/9; F3 (content-addressed units) unapplied in 3a/3b/5; F2 (redaction) unapplied in 3a/3b/6/10; F5 (ACL revocation inventory) partial in 6/7; F4 (filtered ANN) unapplied in 5; F6 (provenance/taint) entirely absent from 11; F12 partially applied in 3c (extended by layer 4). No PR description carries the §6-required amendment checklist. Per-layer evidence lives in the evidence file's layer entries.

## Dominant themes

1. **Purge/redaction is structurally impossible in the shipped schemas** (layers 3a, 3b, 9, 10): four independent mechanisms — the revision-immutability trigger + RESTRICT chains, artifact immutability, citation RESTRICT FKs, and the immutable payload-purge deadline — each forecloses the §4.8 cascade that D7 makes foundational.
2. **ACL enforcement leaks at the edges** (layers 6, 7): fail-open member-removal preservation, ignored privacy conversions, no collaborator-removal event path, non-monotonic ACL sealing, empty-grant reseals.
3. **Ordering ties lose real edits** (layers 3c, 9, 10): `<=` staleness on equal timestamps, hash tie-breaks, per-delivery random idempotency keys.
4. **Plan operating model** (PLAN/strategy rows): unschedulable gates, zero deployable surface, calendar gates inside stacked PRs, missing eval thresholds and cost numbers — addressed by decisions D17–D20 in this PR.
5. **Reviewer service defects** (service rows): fixes are **open, not merged** — the correctness agent + eval-gate expansion in [#34577](https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/openrouter-web/pull/34577), the lifecycle/spend/idempotency races in [#34570](https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/openrouter-web/pull/34570) + [#34571](https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/openrouter-web/pull/34571), responder sanitization in [#34571](https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/openrouter-web/pull/34571), and log redaction in [#33887](https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/openrouter-web/pull/33887). Each row's disposition names its PR; a row is settled only when that PR merges. Fingerprint path-drift (G-rows under "review quality") and memory disposition poisoning remain undisposed.

## Refuted findings

Two serious claims did not survive adversarial verification and are excluded from the G-rows; both are documented with their full refutation reasoning in the [evidence file](./review-findings-2026-08-17-evidence.md#refuted-findings) because they are the objections most likely to resurface (same convention as the F-register):

- **R1 (layer 1, #32299):** "contract-valid upserts lacking sourceVersion/sourceUpdatedAt can defeat the storage-layer monotonic stale guard" — refuted: layer 3c hard-rejects such deliveries (`DeliveryOrderingMissing`, with a test), the architecture places the requirement at the storage layer (§3.4), and F12 already dispositioned the placement.
- **R2 (layer 3b, #32344):** "state='failed' deliveries are permanently unrecoverable" — refuted: reconcile deliveries are the in-contract replay path (fresh deliveryId; §8 mandates periodic reconciliation), so transiently-failed events converge on the next sweep.
