# Connect Customer Organizations

The Connect customer table follows parent-owned clients and partner-provisioned grants, never the provisioning key or the viewer's customer-org membership. Visibility requires an active, non-deleted client, an active non-revoked grant, and a non-deleted, non-banned organization (including active account-ban restrictions). Read this authorization relationship from the primary.

`users.first_name` is the stored organization display name. Do not strip a parent prefix. Partner-supplied customer IDs are not yet recorded separately from the provisioning replay key; do not expose or search `grants.external_id` as customer metadata until that contract is resolved.

Users means current organization memberships excluding the managed owner, not recently active users. The partner provisioning flow records that identity in the customer organization's `users.creator_user_id`; exclude that exact membership, not all admins and not a fixed subtraction from the count. The Clerk private provisioning marker is temporary and cleared after success, so it is not an identification source. If the recorded creator is missing, return an unavailable user count rather than guessing. Created means the earliest partner-provisioned grant creation for that parent/customer pair. Lifetime creation counts deduplicate that pair across all lifecycle states, independently of search and pagination; the weekly subset starts Monday at 00:00 UTC. Historical counts do not grant access to revoked, banned, or deleted customers.

## Acting as a customer

A Connect partner admin acts as a customer organization without a membership in it. `getConnectActingCustomer` is the eligibility rule and runs on the primary for every acting request: an active `org:admin` membership in the parent, an active parent-owned client with an active partner-provisioned grant and no competing partner, non-deleted and non-banned parent, customer and user (a person, not an organization account) with no active account ban, and a customer with no HIPAA workspace (soft-deleted included), no HIPAA default, and no unexpired HIPAA entitlement. The active-partner-grant and active-account-ban conditions come from `activePartnerGrantExpression` and `activeAccountBanExpression`, which the customer list uses too; change them there, not inline. Callers check the Connect entitlement separately with the shared entitlement helpers. Never cache the answer across requests, and never add this lookup to inference authentication; inference rejects the acting cookie instead. The acting workspace reads (`listConnectActingWorkspaces`, `activeConnectActingWorkspacesQuery`) are an authorization set and run on the primary.
