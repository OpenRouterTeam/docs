## PDF URLs  
Now you can send PDF links directly to OpenRouter—no base64 encoding needed.

- Works with any LLM on OpenRouter  
- Learn how in the [PDF URL Docs](https://openrouter.ai/docs/features/multimodal/pdfs#using-pdf-urls)
![PDF Input Preview](https://openrouter.ai/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/0f006f6e-2203-43fb-46a6-dd66a2775600/public)
This makes it easier than ever to integrate document workflows into your apps without the hassle of manual encoding.
