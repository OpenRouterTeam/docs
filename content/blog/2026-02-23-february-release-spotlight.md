Our monthly Product Spotlight highlights a few of our biggest releases from the past month.

## Benchmarks on Model Pages
Every model page now shows industry-standard benchmark scores powered by Artificial Analysis: programming, math, science, long-context reasoning, and more. See how models actually perform before you pick one. See an [example with Claude Sonnet 4.6](https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6/benchmarks)

![Benchmarks Screen Capture](https://imagedelivery.net/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/3d622877-bdd1-4d93-5819-c2facef53b00/public)

[Announcement](https://x.com/OpenRouter/status/2024172341190938958)

## Effective Pricing
Model pages now have an Effective Pricing tab showing what you actually pay per provider, including tiered pricing. Full cost transparency before you route. See an [example with GLM-5](https://openrouter.ai/z-ai/glm-5/pricing)

![Pricing Screen Capture](https://imagedelivery.net/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/e7f31aed-9584-40d6-43cf-fd730065f000/public)

[Announcement](https://x.com/OpenRouter/status/2025988709401743395)

## Rankings & Leaderboard Updates
The Rankings page now includes benchmark scatter charts and expanded tables. Plus, long-context generations are surging. See what's trending for 100K–1M token requests.

![Rankings Screen Capture](https://imagedelivery.net/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/3343c0ac-7d7c-46bb-774d-b14f71a8d700/public)

[Announcement](https://x.com/OpenRouter/status/2024526397838885160)

## Free Router
New router: openrouter/free — the easiest way to route to all free LLMs, automatically selected for compatibility with your request. See the [top free models here](https://openrouter.ai/openrouter/free)
